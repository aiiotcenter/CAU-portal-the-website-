Mandatory Registration, VAT & Social Activity Fee

Registration, VAT, and student social activity fees are excluded from gross tuition rates. All enrolled students contribute an annual fee of $600 USD covering registration maintenance, campus Wi-Fi, lab equipment usage, library access, and athletic facilities.

Payment Guidelines & Methods

To maintain official active student status and course registration, tuition installments must be completed at the start of each semester during the announced payment window.

Accepted Currency

Tuition amounts designated in USD must be deposited in USD or local equivalent through official bank channels.

Virtual POS & Credit Cards

Support for Visa and MasterCard with 3D Secure verification. Contact admission@cau.edu.tr to request a secured online payment link.

Official Confirmation Receipt

Always include your Full Name and CAU Student / Application ID in the wire transfer description to ensure instantaneous accounting confirmation.

Mandatory Payment Deposits

Payment of the initial deposit is required to obtain your official unconditional Acceptance Letter for visa processing and seat confirmation:

Associate & Bachelor Programs: $1,250 USD Master's Programs: $1,250 USD Ph.D. Programs: $1,500 USD Dentistry Program: $2,500 USD

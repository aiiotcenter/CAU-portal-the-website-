Applying to CAU is completed entirely through our online portal. The standard application workflow involves four simple steps:

  • Select your intended degree program and intake semester (Fall, Spring, or Summer).
  • Complete the basic personal profile and upload scanned academic credentials.
  • Receive your official Conditional Acceptance Letter (CAL) within 2 business days upon verification.
  • Make the advance tuition deposit to secure your seat and receive your Unconditional Acceptance Letter (EAL) for visa clearance.

There is no non-refundable application submission fee for international students.

For Undergraduate (Bachelor's & Associate) applications:

  • High School Diploma or Secondary School Leaving Certificate (WAEC/NECO, Tawjihi, Baccalaureate, GCE A-Levels, etc.).
  • Official high school academic transcript containing all senior secondary grades.
  • Copy of valid international passport (minimum 12 months remaining validity).
  • One recent passport-size biometric photograph.

For Graduate (Master's) programs, an accredited Bachelor's degree certificate and undergraduate transcript (minimum 2.25 CGPA or equivalent) are additionally required.

CAU operates on a rolling admissions model with two primary academic semesters:

  • Fall Semester: Applications open January 15; regular deadline is September 30 (Classes commence mid-October).
  • Spring Semester: Applications open October 15; regular deadline is February 25 (Classes commence early March).

International students requiring a Turkish transit visa are encouraged to submit applications at least 4 to 6 weeks before semester registration begins.

Once all required digital documents are uploaded and verified, the International Admissions Committee typically issues a Conditional Acceptance Letter (CAL) within 24 to 48 hours. The letter outlines your program of study, granted scholarship percentage, and deposit instructions.

Yes. Every admitted international student is granted a guaranteed 50% tuition scholarship by the CAU Board of Trustees upon acceptance. This brings annual undergraduate tuition down to approximately $2,800 to $3,200 per academic year across most faculties (excluding Dentistry).

Additional academic merit scholarships (up to 75% or 100%) and sibling discounts (10% extra) are reviewed by the Scholarship Evaluation Board based on exceptional grades.

Yes. Following the initial deposit required for the official acceptance and visa letter, registered students may divide the remaining annual fee into two semester installments (Fall and Spring). Special monthly payment plans can be requested directly through the Accounting & Financial Affairs Directorate.

Tuition payments must be transferred through official international SWIFT wire transfer to our designated university bank accounts at Garanti Bank BBVA (Northern Cyprus Branch). All payments must include the student's full name and Application / Student ID number in the transaction description.

Detailed bank account routing coordinates and IBAN details are printed on every official Acceptance Letter and accessible on our Tuition & Fees page.

The annual $600 administrative fee covers essential student services throughout the entire academic year:

  • Campus health center emergency clinic consultations and basic medical services.
  • Comprehensive on-campus student activity club funding, cultural festivals, and excursions.
  • Unlimited high-speed campus Wi-Fi, computer labs, and digital research library database subscriptions.
  • Free university shuttle bus transportation between Kyrenia campus and surrounding student hubs.

The Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus does not issue entry visas prior to arrival for the majority of nationalities. Upon landing at Ercan International Airport (ECN), you present your official CAU Acceptance Letter and proof of tuition payment to border immigration officers, who stamp a temporary student entry permit (usually 30 to 60 days).

Important Note on Transit Visa: Because all flights to Northern Cyprus transit via Turkey (Istanbul or Ankara), students from countries requiring a visa for Turkey must obtain an official Turkish Transit Visa from their nearest Turkish Embassy prior to departure.

Yes. The International Office operates a complimentary airport reception service for all newly arriving students landing at Ercan International Airport (ECN). Students must submit their flight arrival itinerary to international@cau.edu.tr at least 3 days in advance.

Upon completing on-campus registration at the Registrar's Office, the International Office guides every student through the Ministry of Interior online residency portal. The process includes a mandatory health screening (tuberculosis test, hepatitis, and HIV blood screening at an authorized local hospital) and payment of the annual government residency fee. Once verified, your digital permit is linked to your passport.

The majority of degree programs in Engineering, Computer Science, Business Administration, International Relations, and Tourism are taught 100% in English. Specific programs such as Turkish Law or Turkish Language Teaching are taught in Turkish.

Students who do not hold an internationally recognized English language certificate (such as CSU EPE B1, TOEFL iBT 60, IELTS 5.0, Duolingo 85 for undergraduate; or CSU EPE B2, TOEFL iBT 65, IELTS 5.5, Duolingo 90 for graduate) sit for the CAU English Proficiency Exam upon arrival. Those requiring improvement enroll in the intensive English Preparatory School (1 or 2 semesters) before commencing degree coursework.

Free university shuttle buses operate regular routes between student residential areas and the main campus.

Cyprus Aydin University is fully chartered and accredited by YÖDAK (Higher Education Planning, Evaluation, Accreditation and Coordination Council of Northern Cyprus) and recognized by YÖK (Council of Higher Education of the Republic of Turkey). Degree diplomas conform to the European Higher Education Area (EHEA) standards and the European Credit Transfer System (ECTS), enabling global recognition for graduate study and employment.

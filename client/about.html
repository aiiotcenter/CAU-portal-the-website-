Our
University.

A young, dynamic higher education institution located in the Mediterranean port city of Kyrenia.

Kyrenia Campus

Close to Kyrenia city center & harbor, Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus.

● Established in 2013, Cyprus Aydin University rapidly earned its distinguished place among the young, innovative higher education institutions of the Mediterranean. Welcoming its inaugural cohort in 2014, CAU has flourished into a vibrant international academic community hosting students from Turkey, Northern Cyprus, and more than 60 nations worldwide.

Our educational programmes, delivered in both Turkish and English, adhere strictly to European Higher Education Area (EHEA) standards, equipping graduates to excel in competitive global industries.

Smart Interactive Classrooms

Equipped with modern educational technology including smart boards, interactive projection displays, and collaborative multimedia workstations to ensure an engaging, student-centered learning environment.

Practical Laboratories & Studios

Over 10 specialized on-campus ateliers and laboratories—spanning phantom-head dental clinics, advanced robotics suites, simulated moot courtrooms, and architectural drafting studios.

Towards a Bright Future: CAU & IAU

Cyprus Aydın University and Istanbul Aydın University share an enduring academic alliance, opening world-class dual degree pathways, cross-border research, and global exchange opportunities.

Global Sister Institution Istanbul Aydın University (IAU) · Istanbul, Turkey

Pioneering Higher Education in the Heart of Eurasia

Founded in 2003, Istanbul Aydın University (IAU) has emerged as one of Türkiye’s most sought-after universities. Located on Istanbul’s European side, IAU boasts over 50,000 active students, including more than 8,500 international students from 124 countries. With 17 faculties and schools, IAU achieves an impressive 83.6% job placement rate post-graduation.

As proud partners, Cyprus Aydın University benefits immensely from this collaboration—enhancing academic opportunities, joint research initiatives, faculty exchanges, and dual-campus clinical training (including our premier 5-year Dentistry pathway: 3 years at CAU in Kyrenia followed by 2 clinical years at IAU in Istanbul).

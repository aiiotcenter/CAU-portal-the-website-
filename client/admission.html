Associate & Bachelor Programs — Required Documents

All documents must be in English or Turkish, or accompanied by an official notarized translation.

High School Diploma

Original secondary completion certificate translated into Turkish or English.

High School Transcript

Official complete grade sheet covering all secondary school years (translated into TR/EN).

Passport & Residence Permit

Valid passport identification copy, and existing residence permit if already in TRNC.

4 Passport-Sized Photos

Standard biometric student photos taken within the last six months.

Master’s Programs — Required Documents

For postgraduate applicants pursuing thesis or non-thesis master’s degrees.

Bachelor’s Degree Diploma

Certified undergraduate graduation diploma translated into Turkish or English.

Bachelor’s Transcript

Complete official university transcript detailing all semesters, credits, and CGPA.

Passport & Residence Permit

Clear scan of international passport ID page and TRNC permit (if available).

4 Passport-Sized Photos

Official passport-format photographs for student identification records.

Doctoral Programs (Ph.D.) — Required Documents

For research candidates applying for advanced doctoral scholarship and dissertation tracks.

Master’s Degree Diploma

Certified postgraduate master’s certificate translated into Turkish or English.

Master’s Transcript

Complete master’s coursework transcript with thesis defense verification.

Passport & Residence Permit

Valid passport biographical page scan and existing TRNC permit (if available).

4 Passport-Sized Photos

Official passport-sized biometric photographs for doctoral academic file.

Eligibility Criteria

Students who hold an Associate Degree, Higher National Diploma (HND), or who are currently enrolled in good academic standing at another recognized higher education institution are welcome to apply for advanced standing.

Transfer Application Checklist

University Official Transcripts

Complete certified transcripts showing all semester courses, ECTS or credit weights, and letter grades achieved.

Detailed Course Descriptions / Syllabi

Official course outlines from your previous university catalogue to facilitate faculty credit-exemption matching.

Secondary Certificate Copy

High school graduation diploma and passport identification copy.

Departments & Faculties

Cyprus Aydin University is organized into 7 specialized faculties offering 48 innovative programmes across undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral disciplines. Each faculty combines rigorous academic instruction, accredited curricula, state-of-the-art laboratory facilities, and international career preparation in the coastal university city of Kyrenia.

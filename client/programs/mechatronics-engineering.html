About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The Bachelor of Science in Mechatronics Engineering combines mechanical engineering, electronics, computer science, and control theory into a unified discipline.

Students design robotic manipulators, automated manufacturing lines, drone guidance systems, and medical mechatronics using industrial PLCs, sensory arrays, and embedded microprocessors.

With extensive laboratory coursework in robotics kinematics and CNC manufacturing, graduates lead automation across the automotive, aerospace, healthcare, and smart consumer technology industries.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

Robotics & Autonomous Navigation

Kinematic modeling, trajectory planning, LiDAR obstacle avoidance, and computer vision.

Industrial PLC & Automation

Programming programmable logic controllers, pneumatic logic, and SCADA industrial interfaces.

Sensory Fusion & Actuators

Interfacing encoders, load cells, stepper/servo motors, and embedded PID controllers.

Laboratories & Studios

Infrastructure

Autonomous Robotics & Vision Lab

Multi-axis robotic arms, mobile rover platforms, and stereo vision cameras.

PLC & Pneumatics Automation Bench

Siemens industrial PLCs, pneumatic cylinders, electro-valves, and conveyor simulators.

CNC & Prototyping Workshop

Computerized milling machines, 3D printers, and precision mechanical assembly stations.

Sensory Measurement & Actuation Suite

Optical encoders, torque sensors, strain gauges, and dynamometer motor testers.

Career Opportunities

Graduate Pathways

Graduates of the Mechatronics Engineering programme at Cyprus Aydin University enter high-demand careers across their industry. The CAU Career Center provides active career guidance, internship placements, and corporate recruitment networking.

Robotics Design Engineer

Developing automated robotic arms, agricultural drones, and autonomous guided vehicles (AGVs).

Industrial Automation Specialist

Programming assembly line PLCs, SCADA networks, and smart factory sensors.

Automotive & Autonomous Systems Engineer

Designing electric vehicle drive controllers, ADAS sensor fusion, and powertrain electronics.

Medical Mechatronics Specialist

Building robotic surgical instruments, automated prosthetic limbs, and diagnostic devices.

Aerospace Systems Specialist

Engineering flight control actuators, gimbal stabilizers, and unmanned aerial vehicle autopilots.

Embedded Firmware Developer

Writing motor driver firmware, sensor calibration libraries, and real-time control loops.

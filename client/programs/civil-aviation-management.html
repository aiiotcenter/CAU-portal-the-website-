About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The Bachelor of Science in Civil Aviation Management prepares future airline executives and airport operations managers in the dynamic commercial aerospace industry.

Students gain mastery in air transport economics, airport terminal capacity planning, flight crew scheduling, cargo logistics, international air law, and aviation safety management systems (SMS).

Benefiting from Northern Cyprus’s strategic aviation connections, students visit international airports, flight simulator centres, and air traffic control towers during their studies.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

Airline Operations & Fleet Scheduling

Optimizing flight routes, crew rotations, fuel efficiency, and fleet turnaround logistics.

ICAO & IATA Regulatory Compliance

International civil aviation protocols, bilateral air service agreements, and security mandates.

Airport Terminal Management

Passenger check-in throughput, runway slot allocations, baggage logistics, and retail concession planning.

Laboratories & Studios

Infrastructure

Flight Dispatch & Operations Simulator

Computerized flight planning software calculating fuel burns, wind vectors, and alternate airports.

Airport Passenger Service Simulation

Mock check-in counters with airline reservation systems (Amadeus / Sabre).

Air Traffic Simulation Room

Visual tower radar displays and terminal airspace traffic flow monitors.

Air Cargo Logistics Center

Unit load device (ULD) planning software and weight/balance calculation workstations.

Career Opportunities

Graduate Pathways

Graduates of the Civil Aviation Management programme at Cyprus Aydin University enter high-demand careers across their industry. The CAU Career Center provides active career guidance, internship placements, and corporate recruitment networking.

Airline Operations Supervisor

Managing daily flight dispatch, turnaround coordination, and schedule adjustments.

Airport Terminal Duty Manager

Overseeing passenger terminal services, security checkpoints, and baggage flow.

Aviation Revenue & Pricing Analyst

Modeling seat yield management, booking curves, and competitive ticket pricing.

Air Cargo Logistics Manager

Coordinating international air freight manifests, customs clearance, and courier fleets.

Aviation Safety & Compliance Auditor

Conducting SMS risk assessments and ensuring compliance with ICAO/EASA standards.

Airport Concession & Marketing Director

Negotiating duty-free retail leases, lounge partnerships, and airline landing contracts.

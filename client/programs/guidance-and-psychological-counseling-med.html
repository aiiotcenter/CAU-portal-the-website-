About the Programme

Overview & Vision

GUIDANCE AND PSYCHOLOGICAL COUNSELING (M.Ed.) at Cyprus Aydin University equips students with modern professional skills and academic foundations.

The curriculum integrates contemporary disciplinary methods, laboratory experiments, and industrial project experience in Kyrenia.

Under our distinguished academic faculty, students gain the analytical dexterity and ethical grounding required for successful international careers.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

Advanced Research Methodology

Rigorous quantitative and qualitative methodologies for original scholarly investigation.

Specialized Subject Mastery

Deep dive into contemporary theories, case literature, and advanced domain paradigms.

Academic Publication & Defense

Mentorship towards high-impact international journal submissions and thesis defense.

About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The Bachelor of Science in Nursing (English) at Cyprus Aydin University prepares healthcare professionals who integrate medical science, ethical empathy, and international best practices.

Students train in high-fidelity hospital simulation wards equipped with interactive patient mannequins, practicing wound management, medication calculation, vital sign stabilization, and clinical triage.

With clinical rotations conducted in leading medical centres and regional hospitals, graduates possess the qualifications and international language proficiency to practice globally.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

High-Fidelity Simulation

Patient mannequins with programmable vital signs, respiratory distress, and cardiac arrhythmia.

Clinical Hospital Rotations

Hands-on patient care semesters across emergency wards, ICUs, surgical suites, and pediatrics.

Global Healthcare Standards

English medical terminology aligned with international nursing licensing examinations (NCLEX/OET).

Laboratories & Studios

Infrastructure

Nursing Simulation Ward

Hospital-grade beds, cardiac monitors, intravenous infusion pumps, and clinical dressing carts.

Maternal & Child Health Lab

Infant incubators, neonatal resuscitation models, and birthing simulation mannequins.

Emergency & Trauma Suite

Triage simulation area for crash cart training, defibrillation, and advanced life support (ACLS).

Anatomy & Microbiology Lab

Microscopes, histological preparations, and 3D organ specimens.

Career Opportunities

Graduate Pathways

Graduates of the Nursing (English) programme at Cyprus Aydin University enter high-demand careers across their industry. The CAU Career Center provides active career guidance, internship placements, and corporate recruitment networking.

Registered Clinical Nurse (RN)

Managing patient care plans, medication administration, and vital sign monitoring in hospitals.

Critical Care / ICU Nurse

Delivering continuous intensive care for critically ill patients in trauma and surgical ICUs.

Surgical Theatre Scrub Nurse

Assisting surgeons during operations, maintaining sterile fields, and instrument handling.

Pediatric & Neonatal Nurse

Specialized medical nursing care for premature infants, children, and adolescents.

Community & Public Health Nurse

Coordinating immunization campaigns, maternal education, and chronic disease clinics.

Nurse Manager & Administrator

Supervising ward nursing teams, shift schedules, and hospital quality assurance protocols.

About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The English Preparatory School at Cyprus Aydın University provides comprehensive intensive language instruction designed to transition international students into university degree coursework.

Our curriculum integrates modern communicative teaching methods, multimedia language labs, academic reading, and essay composition in Kyrenia.

Under experienced ESL faculty, students gain the confidence and academic vocabulary necessary to excel in Bachelor's, Master's, and doctoral programs.

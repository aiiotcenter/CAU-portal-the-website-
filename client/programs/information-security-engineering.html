About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The Bachelor of Science in Information Security Engineering trains cybersecurity professionals capable of safeguarding enterprise data, networks, and cloud platforms against sophisticated global threats.

Students gain deep technical competency in ethical hacking, penetration testing, cryptographic mathematics, network defense, secure coding, digital forensics, and cloud infrastructure compliance.

In our isolated Cyber Sandbox, students simulate real-time cyber attacks and defense drills (Red Team vs. Blue Team), mastering the tools required for immediate professional accreditation.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

Ethical Hacking & Penetration Testing

Vulnerability assessment, exploit development, web app auditing, and reverse engineering.

Cyber Defense & Threat Intelligence

SIEM monitoring, intrusion detection, firewall configuration, and automated incident response.

Applied Cryptography & Forensics

Public-key cryptography, zero-knowledge proofs, blockchain security, and digital evidence recovery.

Career Opportunities

Graduate Pathways

Graduates of the Information Security Engineering programme at Cyprus Aydin University enter high-demand careers across their industry. The CAU Career Center provides active career guidance, internship placements, and corporate recruitment networking.

Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker

Identifying exploitable vulnerabilities in client networks, APIs, and cloud services.

SOC Security Analyst

Monitoring enterprise traffic, mitigating real-time cyber attacks, and conducting triage.

Digital Forensics Specialist

Recovering digital evidence, analyzing ransomware artifacts, and providing legal reports.

DevSecOps & Cloud Security Engineer

Hardening Kubernetes pipelines, Docker containers, and automated security code scans.

Cyber Risk & Compliance Officer

Aligning enterprise architecture with ISO 27001, GDPR, and PCI-DSS compliance frameworks.

Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

Directing strategic enterprise security policy and risk management for corporations.

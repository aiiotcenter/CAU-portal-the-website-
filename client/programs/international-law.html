About the Programme

Overview & Vision

The Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) in International Law at Cyprus Aydin University prepares global legal practitioners skilled in transnational litigation, cross-border commerce, and multilateral diplomacy.

Taught entirely in English, students analyze international public treaties, maritime disputes, commercial arbitration rules (ICC, LCIA), foreign direct investment treaties, and human rights charters.

Students participate in international moot court tournaments (such as the Jessup and Willem C. Vis competitions) in our campus courtroom, cultivating cross-border advocacy mastery.

Core Competencies

Learning Pillars

Transnational Commercial Arbitration

International contract drafting, investor-state dispute settlement (ISDS), and enforcement.

Law of the Sea & Maritime Law

Exclusive economic zones (EEZ), maritime boundary delimitation, and shipping transport law.

International Human Rights & Treaties

Geneva Conventions, European Court of Human Rights jurisprudence, and international criminal law.

Curriculum Road Map

4 Years

Year 1: Foundations of Law

60 ECTS
  • Introduction to Legal Systems & Common Law
  • Constitutional Law & Principles of Governance
  • Public International Law I
  • Legal Research, Analysis & Citation
  • History of International Law
  • Foundations of Torts & Contracts

Year 2: Public International Law

60 ECTS
  • Public International Law II: State Responsibility
  • Law of the Sea & Maritime Law
  • International Human Rights Law
  • International Organizations (UN / EU / WTO)
  • Contract Law in International Commerce
  • Comparative Constitutional Systems

Year 3: Commercial & Dispute Law

60 ECTS
  • International Commercial Arbitration
  • Private International Law (Conflict of Laws)
  • World Trade Organization (WTO) Law
  • International Environmental Law
  • International Criminal & Humanitarian Law
  • Drafting International Legal Instruments

Year 4: Capstone & Practice

60 ECTS
  • Senior International Moot Court Capstone I & II
  • Cross-Border Mergers, Acquisitions & Investment
  • International Intellectual Property Law
  • Diplomatic & Consular Law
  • Mandatory Legal Internship
  • Legal Ethics

Career Opportunities

Graduate Pathways

Graduates of the International Law programme at Cyprus Aydin University enter high-demand careers across their industry. The CAU Career Center provides active career guidance, internship placements, and corporate recruitment networking.

International Legal Counsel

Drafting cross-border commercial joint ventures and compliance policies for multinationals.

Commercial Arbitration Specialist

Representing claimants and respondents in institutional international arbitration proceedings.

Maritime & Shipping Legal Advisor

Advising maritime shipping fleets on charter parties, marine insurance, and boundary claims.

Diplomatic Legal Attaché

Serving foreign ministries and diplomatic delegations on bilateral treaty ratifications.

Human Rights Advocate

Filing complaints before international regional human rights courts and tribunals.

Corporate Compliance Officer

Ensuring global corporate alignment with international anti-money laundering and trade sanctions.

About the Faculty

Overview & Vision

The Faculty of Dentistry at Cyprus Aydin University prepares future dental practitioners through a comprehensive 5-year curriculum integrating fundamental medical sciences, simulation-based manual dexterity training, and advanced clinical patient care.

Under the leadership of renowned oral surgeon and Dean Prof. Dr. Cem Kurtoğlu, the faculty maintains a close student-to-professor ratio. Students learn in state-of-the-art facilities featuring computerized phantom simulation units, digital panoramic radiology, modern sterilization centers, and advanced dental prosthetics laboratories.

Our dental graduates acquire deep expertise across endodontics, prosthodontics, periodontology, oral and maxillofacial surgery, pediatric dentistry, and orthodontics, ensuring readiness for clinical licensing and international medical practice.

Core Academic Strengths

Clinical Excellence

Phantom Head Simulators

Realistic preclinical training units simulating patient head movement, jaw ergonomics, and oral tissue mechanics.

Digital Dental Imaging

Low-dose digital panoramic X-ray systems, 3D intraoral scanners, and computer-assisted diagnostic software.

Global Student Community

Small-cohort teaching with students from over 8 nations, fostering an international clinical mindset.

About the Faculty

Overview & Vision

The Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences at Cyprus Aydin University prepares dynamic graduates who understand the complex economic, psychological, and geopolitical interconnections defining our world.

Under Dean Prof. Dr. Arzu Alvan Bozdereli, the faculty delivers 5 forward-looking Bachelor’s programmes spanning corporate business administration, international relations, clinical and developmental psychology, commercial aviation management, and international banking and finance.

Students participate in interactive seminar discussions, Model United Nations simulations, stock market investment games, and psychological field studies. With modern classrooms situated overlooking the Mediterranean, students benefit from a genuinely international peer community and direct access to academic mentors.

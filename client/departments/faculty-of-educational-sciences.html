About the Faculty

Overview & Vision

The Faculty of Educational Sciences at Cyprus Aydin University prepares educators and psychological counselors who foster intellectual curiosity, emotional well-being, and social development in 21st-century learners.

Under Dean Prof. Dr. Ahmet Pehlivan, the faculty offers 3 distinguished degree programmes: English Language Teaching (ELT), Guidance and Psychological Counselling (PDR), and Turkish Language Teaching. Each programme integrates contemporary cognitive learning theories, developmental psychology, and interactive digital educational technologies.

Our students gain direct classroom mastery in our dedicated Microteaching Studio—where simulated lessons are recorded, analyzed, and refined with faculty mentors—prior to completing mandatory teaching residencies in partner primary and secondary schools.

Core Academic Strengths

Pedagogical Excellence

Microteaching Video Lab

Lesson presentation recording with multi-angle cameras, vocal pacing evaluation, and structured peer critiques.

Counselling Practicum

Individual and group counselling observation suites for mastering developmental and clinical adolescent guidance.

Partner School Residencies

Structured student-teaching semesters in accredited public and international schools across Cyprus.

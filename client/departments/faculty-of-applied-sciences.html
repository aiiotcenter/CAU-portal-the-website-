About the Faculty

Overview & Vision

The Faculty of Applied Sciences at Cyprus Aydin University bridges academic management principles with hands-on technical artistry in our cutting-edge professional culinary suites and hospitality training labs.

Situated in Kyrenia—the undisputed tourism capital of Northern Cyprus—under Dean Prof. Dr. Salih Dinçel, the faculty offers two premier programmes: Gastronomy and Culinary Arts and Tourism and Hospitality Management. Students learn in custom-designed commercial kitchens equipped with individual chef workstations, induction suites, combi-steamers, and patisserie equipment.

Our hospitality management students gain hands-on proficiency in international property management software (Opera / Amadeus), banquet operations, and guest relations. Mandatory summer internships at partner 5-star Mediterranean beachfront resorts provide immediate access to global career placements upon graduation.

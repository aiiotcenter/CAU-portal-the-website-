About the Faculty

Overview & Vision

The Faculty of Engineering and Architecture at Cyprus Aydin University educates engineers, software architects, and designers equipped with cutting-edge theoretical knowledge, problem-solving dexterity, and deep ethical commitment.

Situated in the historic coastal city of Kyrenia, TRNC, the faculty is home to 8 comprehensive undergraduate degree programmes. Our curriculum bridges timeless engineering fundamentals with emerging frontiers, including artificial intelligence, autonomous robotics, cybersecurity, sustainable civil infrastructure, and bioclimatic architecture.

Students learn in modern laboratories, creative drafting ateliers, and smart interactive lecture halls equipped with the latest software tooling (AutoCAD, Revit, MATLAB, Cisco Networking suites, and embedded development boards). Under the leadership of Dean Prof. Dr. Hikmet Ş. Aybar and our international faculty body, students participate in real-world capstone projects, hackathons, and industrial internships from their third year onward.

Pillars of Excellence

Specialised Computing Labs

Continuous atelier culture with personal design desks, 3D printing, laser cutters, and physical structural model workshops.

Industry Capstones

Collaborative graduation projects and internships with regional technology firms, construction conglomerates, and tech incubators.

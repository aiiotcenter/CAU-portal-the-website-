How To Secure Your Scholarship

All scholarships at Cyprus Aydin University follow a transparent, straightforward 4-step evaluation workflow.

STEP 01

Submit Application

Complete the free online admissions portal with your international passport copy, high school transcripts, and any test scores.

STEP 02

Academic Review

Our Admissions Evaluation Committee reviews your grades and SAT scores to determine the highest applicable scholarship tier.

STEP 03

Official Offer Letter

Receive your formal Conditional Acceptance Letter, clearly outlining your awarded scholarship percentage and fixed annual fee.

STEP 04

Deposit & Visa Clearance

Settle your initial deposit to activate your official acceptance code, official scholarship record, and embassy visa confirmation letter.

Scholarship Policies & FAQ

Common questions regarding scholarship renewals, eligibility criteria, and fee structures.

Yes, guaranteed. All undergraduate and vocational scholarships awarded upon admission remain locked for the standard duration of the degree program (4 years for Bachelor's degrees, 2 years for Vocational/Associate degrees, 2 years for Master's programs), provided students maintain good academic standing and observe university conduct regulations.

There is no annual re-application or additional evaluation fee required to sustain your awarded rate.

Under university regulations, scholarships generally cannot be combined or stacked, with one specific exception:

  • Sibling Educational Discount: Concurrently enrolled siblings receive an extra 10% discount stacked on top of the base 70% International Student Regional Grant, bringing total coverage up to 80%.

In all other scenarios, applicants are automatically awarded whichever single eligible scholarship grants the highest financial discount.

Students receiving 100% full tuition waivers have all academic teaching and course instruction costs fully covered. Recipients remain responsible only for mandatory statutory fees:

  • Annual Registration & Student Activity Fee: $350 USD per academic year.
  • Local Educational VAT (5%): Mandated by the TRNC Ministry of National Education.
  • Optional Services: On-campus housing, meal plans, or personal health insurance (if selected).

Recipients of the Presidential 100% Merit Scholarship and Dean's 85% Honors Award are expected to maintain a cumulative grade point average (CGPA) of at least 3.00 out of 4.00 across consecutive academic semesters.

If a student's CGPA temporarily falls below 3.00, they are gracefully transitioned to the guaranteed baseline 70% international grant rather than losing all financial support, and may restore their higher merit tier once their CGPA returns to 3.00+.

Cyprus Aydin University operates on a rolling admissions model for both the Fall (September) and Spring (February) academic intakes.

However, because quotas for full 100% and 85% academic merit scholarships are strictly capped per faculty, awards are distributed on a first-come, first-served basis. Prospective students are strongly encouraged to submit their documents at least 4 to 6 weeks prior to semester start dates.

Student Guidance

Campus Transit Policies & FAQ

Complimentary for All Students

CAU shuttle transportation is 100% free of charge for all registered undergraduate, postgraduate, and language preparatory students, as well as faculty and administrative staff. Simply display your CAU Student ID card upon boarding.

Punctuality & Boarding Advice

Shuttles depart promptly according to scheduled timings. Please arrive at your designated pick-up station at least 5 minutes prior to scheduled departure. Buses do not stop outside officially recognized bus stations.

Boğaz Mountain Pass & Weather

Routes traversing the Beşparmak mountain pass (Gönyeli and Lefkoşa Honda lines) may experience slight buffer adjustments during severe rain or dense morning fog. Drivers prioritize student road safety at all times.

Lost & Found Service

Left a bag, phone, or textbook on the shuttle? All items recovered at the end of each shift are handed directly to the Campus Security Office & Student Affairs at the main administration building.

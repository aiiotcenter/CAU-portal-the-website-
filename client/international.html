Assoc. Prof. Dr. Tuğrul Günay Director of International Office

Cyprus Aydin University

● At CAU, we take immense pride in our vibrant international community, welcoming ambitious students from more than 60 countries across Africa, the Middle East, Central Asia, Europe, and beyond.

Our university has always been student-centered. The International Office is a dedicated place where we prepare our students for high-impact global careers in a multicultural, supportive environment. We offer comprehensive services from initial application to campus orientation and cultural engagement.

"Internationalization is more than mobility — it’s about building bridges of knowledge, culture, and shared purpose. At Cyprus Aydin University, I am committed to fostering global partnerships and empowering students to thrive in a truly interconnected world."
— Assoc. Prof. Dr. Tuğrul Günay, Director of the International Office

Our commitment extends beyond academic coursework — we strongly value global citizenship, sustainability, and collaborative problem-solving. Through cooperative internships, laboratory assistantships, and on-campus work-study placements, our students gain invaluable hands-on experience and graduate with a portfolio of verified achievements.

We are truly honored to welcome you to Northern Cyprus and look forward to accompanying you every step of the way toward your academic degree.

# CAU Homepage Design Direction

## Three possible directions

### Theme Name: Swiss Editorial University
Very clean institutional editorial design built around oversized typography, hairline rules, asymmetric columns, and a controlled coral-red accent.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Mediterranean Campus Journal
A warmer, more photographic direction using sunlit campus imagery, soft sand neutrals, and magazine-like captions.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Research Atlas
A more technical academic interface with indexed sections, dense metadata, navy panels, and a map-inspired visual language.

**Probability:** 0.02

## Chosen approach: Swiss Editorial University

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Typographic Style translated into a contemporary university admissions homepage.

**Core Principles:** Use typographic hierarchy before decoration; preserve a visible structural grid; make coral-red interaction states instantly recognizable; and let whitespace create confidence rather than emptiness.

**Color Philosophy:** The page is primarily white and black to communicate clarity, academic rigor, and openness. Coral red (#f04432) is reserved for action, energy, and moments of institutional pride. Deep navy (#082d70) anchors the inquiry CTA and ties back to the university seal.

**Layout Paradigm:** An editorial split-screen rhythm. Each major section uses a left narrative rail and a right content field, with thin rules and intentionally uneven column weights. On smaller screens the split becomes a calm vertical sequence.

**Signature Elements:** Coral-red action bars and buttons; thin gray rules that reveal the underlying grid; and image cards with small white arrow badges.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Interaction should feel direct and useful. Filters expose content without page reloads, nav links scroll to real sections, the hero rotates through messages, and the inquiry form gives immediate inline feedback.

**Animation:** Use short 160–260ms ease-out transitions for buttons, cards, and nav. Reveal sections with a restrained upward fade only when motion is allowed. No decorative looping motion.

**Typography System:** Use Space Grotesk for display and headings, with a system sans stack for body copy. Headlines are uppercase with tight line-height; body copy is compact, gray, and readable.

**Brand Essence:** A clear, internationally minded university homepage for prospective students who want a future-facing education without losing human warmth. Personality: rigorous, optimistic, welcoming.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are confident and plainspoken. CTAs are active and specific. Example lines: “Find the programme that moves you forward.” and “Can’t find what you are looking for?”

**Wordmark & Logo:** Use a circular seal-inspired geometric mark with a coral diamond in a navy ring, paired with a text wordmark in a restrained grotesk.

**Signature Brand Color:** CAU Coral #f04432.

## Style Decisions

Hero copy is active, specific, and admissions-facing; it avoids generic welcome, future, or brightness language. Campus photography follows a bright editorial-documentary direction with composed campus, student, and faculty scenes rather than casual snapshots. CAU Coral #f04432 is reserved for primary actions, active states, key numerals, and institutional emphasis; white space, black type, rules, and navy provide the quiet structural work.

Secondary headings, metadata, controls, and captions deliberately use calmer editorial weight and stricter rule-based grouping so primary admissions headlines retain visual authority. Rounded, dashboard-like UI surfaces are avoided on the homepage in favor of flatter controls, square grids, and visible structural rules.
